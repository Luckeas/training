    3 · The plan

    Your 2-minute race plan

    1. First 10 strokes

      All out

      Go all out to reach max speed. This is your peak.

    2. Next 5 seconds

      Settle

      Move straight to your target pace with long strokes.

    3. The body of the race

      Hold

      Hold about 20 seconds slower than your peak. Peak at 1:29? Hold 1:48 to 1:52.

    4. Last 20 seconds

      Empty the tank

      Don't rush into quicker strokes. Think long, powerful strokes: the wheel spins faster and your rate comes up on its own.

    In the tests I've tracked, men and women hold about 21 seconds off their peak through the body of the race. The best pacers hold 10 to 15. Fly and die, and it's 30 or more.

    Only compare your numbers on the same machine. Two PERGs can read very differently.
