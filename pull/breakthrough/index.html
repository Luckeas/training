Why the wall at 15?

Most of us keep adding sets and reps, and hope the number moves. But your body has already adapted to that. To keep progressing, it needs a fresh stimulus.

2 · The three changes

You've probably never tried the last one

1

Switch grips

Hit your back from new angles. About 70% regular grip, sometimes a bit wider or closer. The rest chin-ups and neutral, by feel.

And chalk upUse more than you think, so your back gives out before your hands. No chalk allowed at your gym? Try liquid chalk.
2

Add weight

Heavier for lower reps builds strength. Lighter for higher reps builds endurance, without maxing out every set.

HowHeavy: 1 to 2 sets of 3 to 6. Lighter: 8 to 12. Then: Conti sets at bodyweight (see 3), with a new grip.
3

The big one · my own method

Conti sets

Conti is short for continuous: as many reps as you can, with no pause at the bottom. Push that number up every week. On test day, you can usually add another 50%: 20 Conti reps means about 30.

Your Conti numberWrite it down every time. The calculator turns it into your test-day max.

3 · Your 5 weeks

Build, deload, retest

Three gym days: heavy, light and medium. Three weeks of building, a deload, then a retest. Pick a day.

This is your pull-up work, not a full workout. Add your accessory work (section 4) and train the rest of your body.

4 · The sets

The 4 sets in your plan

Warm up with banded scap pulls, leaning back. Chest facing the bar, elbows slightly forward. It frees up your shoulders so they move smoothly.

  1. Weighted pull-ups, heavy

    Strength · a weight where every rep is hard but strict
    1–2sets3–6reps

  2. Weighted pull-ups, lighter

    Endurance, without maxing out every set
    2sets8–12reps

  3. My own method

    Conti sets

    Bodyweight · as many reps as you can, no pause at the bottom
    2–4sets

  4. Threshold set

    Bodyweight · once, on the light day
    • Pull continuous reps until you can't.
    • Then go past failure: keep going with partial reps until you're just dead hanging.
    • Then keep dead hanging until your grip gives out.
    1set

Your accessory work

Add a few of these after your pull-ups, however they fit your week.

  • Lat pulldown, V-grip: the best variation to really target your lats for pull-ups
  • Bench pull: lats, traps, biceps, forearms
  • One-arm row: lats, shoulder blades, forearms
  • Body rows and holds: all of the above, plus connective tissue
  • Hammer curls: brachialis, forearms
