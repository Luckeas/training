Who it's for

Every paddler who wants more speed

It's not about going hardcore. It's a clear plan to get faster, look and feel stronger, and a crew of paddlers sharing the work.

  • You paddle dragon boat and you have a gymRec crew, club crew or national team, at home or abroad.

  • Any level, any ageBrand new to the gym? You start with a 12-week beginner program. Over 40, a student, or chasing the national team? Your program is built for where you are.

Questions

Before you start

What do I need?

A regular gym and your phone. Tell me what your gym has and I build around it.

I've never lifted before. Is it for me?

Yes. If you're brand new to the gym, you start with Foundations, a 12-week beginner program, then move onto the main program.

How many days a week?

Usually 3 gym days a week, planned around your practices and your season.

I'm not in Canada. Does it work?

Yes, it's fully online. Checkout shows US dollars, and your new week arrives at midnight in your own time zone.

Is it in French?

Yes. The app, this page and my answers are in English and French.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from your billing page. No contract. If you cancel during your free week, you're not charged.
